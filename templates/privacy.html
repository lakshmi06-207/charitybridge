Privacy Policy

CharityBridge respects your privacy and aims to protect the information you provide while using the platform.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number and information submitted through donation, volunteer, or help-request forms.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide CharityBridge services, manage accounts, process requests, and improve the platform.

3. Account Security

Users should keep their login credentials secure and should not share their passwords with others.

4. Information Protection

CharityBridge takes reasonable measures to protect user information stored within the system.

5. Third Parties

Personal information should not be shared unnecessarily through the platform.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the CharityBridge team.
